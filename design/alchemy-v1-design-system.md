# alchemy-v1 Anchor Design System

Source of truth: `design/reference_alchemy_report_anchor_v1.png`.

## 1. Page Background

- Color: warm ochre parchment, moving from pale honey near the upper center to deeper amber at the lower page.
- Texture: dense fine square grid plus faint horizontal scratches; the page must feel like printed alchemical paper, not a flat gradient.
- Density: high but low-contrast. Texture is visible across the full page and especially in open lower areas.
- Decorative layer: subtle radial highlights, corner color blocks, thin glyph line near the footer, and a translucent alchemical diagram when useful.
- Ornament placement: fixed to the page frame. The main ornaments live top-right behind the DAO seal, bottom-left near the footer, and in small corner brackets inside the border.

## 2. Frame System

- Outer border: hard rectangular A4 frame with square corners, dark brown outer stroke, and warm gold companion stroke.
- Inner border: inset rectangular frame about 5-8 mm from page edge, with multiple thin strokes rather than one thick border.
- Corner ornaments: asymmetric L-shaped brackets in the upper-left/lower-right, plus dark brown square blocks top-right and bottom-left.
- Edge spacing: content begins inside the inner frame with roughly 17-20 mm page padding; footer stays inside the frame, not outside the page.

## 3. Header System

- Title position: upper-left, large serif display, aligned to the content column.
- Subtitle position: directly under the title, small brown serif text.
- DAO seal position: upper-right, overlapping a dark decorative square; seal is circular with thin rings and a small inner diamond/triangle mark.
- Divider line: thin brown horizontal rule below the page title, spanning most of the content width.

## 4. White Text Inserts

- Allowed sizes: primary letter panel, medium remedy note, small side note, wide conclusion/prescription panel.
- Allowed positions: embedded within the parchment field, offset from other inserts, never filling the whole center page.
- Border style: 1 px warm brown stroke with a light inner highlight.
- Shadow style: low right/down offset in translucent brown; no floating dashboard elevation.
- Rule: a white insert is never the whole center page. The parchment substrate must remain visible around and between inserts.

## 5. Page Layout Types

- Diagnosis page: one large vertical letter insert on the left, small metric inserts on the right, faint alchemical diagram behind the side column.
- Remedy-set map page: vertical ritual map with staggered remedy inserts connected by an axis/glyph layer. No equal 2x2 grid.
- Prescription page: compact ritual chain near the top, then a wide embedded prescription insert with nested warm sub-inserts.
- Integration page: flowing stacked message inserts and a final formula band, with enough parchment visible to keep the booklet feeling.

## 6. Forbidden Layouts

- No 2x2 grid.
- No dashboard cards.
- No blank white page.
- No landscape blocks.
- No contact sheet inside a page.
